# QuizThreads - User Guide

## Welcome to QuizThreads! 🎉

QuizThreads is a platform where creators can build engaging quizzes from their Threads posts and followers can test their knowledge.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an Account
1. Click **"Sign Up"** in the navigation bar
2. Enter your username, email, and password
3. Click **"Sign Up"** to create your account
4. You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your profile

### 2. Login
1. Click **"Login"** in the navigation bar
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

## For Creators

### Creating a Quiz from Threads Posts

1. **Navigate to Profile**
   - Click on your avatar in the top right
   - Select "Profile" from the dropdown

2. **Go to Create Quiz Tab**
   - Click on the "Create Quiz" tab
   - Select "From Threads Posts"

3. **Enter Quiz Details**
   - Give your quiz a catchy title

4. **Fetch Threads Posts**
   - Enter a Threads username
   - Click "Fetch Posts"
   - Select the posts you want to include in your quiz

5. **Create Questions**
   - Click "Add Question"
   - Enter your question text
   - Fill in 4 answer options
   - Select the correct answer
   - Repeat for more questions

6. **Save Your Quiz**
   - Review your questions
   - Click "Save Quiz"
   - Your quiz is now live!

### Creating a Manual Quiz

1. **Navigate to Profile → Create Quiz**
2. **Select "Manual Creation" Tab**
3. **Enter Quiz Title**
4. **Add Questions**
   - Click "Add Question"
   - Enter question text
   - Add 4 answer options
   - Select correct answer
5. **Save Quiz**

### Managing Your Quizzes

1. **View Your Quizzes**
   - Go to Profile → "My Quizzes" tab
   - See all quizzes you've created

2. **Preview a Quiz**
   - Click "Preview" button on any quiz card
   - Test your quiz before sharing

3. **Delete a Quiz**
   - Click the delete icon (trash can)
   - Confirm deletion

## For Quiz Takers

### Taking a Quiz

1. **Browse Available Quizzes**
   - Visit the Home page
   - Browse through available quizzes

2. **Start a Quiz**
   - Click "Take Quiz" on any quiz card
   - Enter your name when prompted

3. **Answer Questions**
   - Read each question carefully
   - Select your answer
   - Click "Next" to continue

4. **View Results**
   - See your score at the end
   - Check which answers were correct

### Viewing the Leaderboard

1. **Navigate to Results**
   - Click "Results" in the navigation bar
   - Or click "View Leaderboard" on the home page

2. **See Rankings**
   - Top 3 users displayed on podium
   - Full rankings list below
   - Rankings based on total questions answered

## Account Settings

### Update Profile

1. **Go to Settings**
   - Click your avatar → "Settings"

2. **Edit Information**
   - Update username or email
   - Click "Save Changes"

### Change Password

1. **In Settings Page**
2. **Enter Current Password**
3. **Enter New Password**
4. **Confirm New Password**
5. **Click "Change Password"**

### Notification Preferences

1. **In Settings Page**
2. **Toggle Notifications**
   - Enable/disable notifications
   - Enable/disable email updates

### Delete Account

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone!

1. **Scroll to "Danger Zone"**
2. **Click "Delete Account"**
3. **Confirm deletion**
4. **Your account and all quizzes will be permanently deleted**

## Tips for Success

### For Creators

✅ **Create Engaging Titles**
- Use catchy, descriptive titles
- Make it clear what the quiz is about

✅ **Write Clear Questions**
- Be specific and unambiguous
- Avoid trick questions

✅ **Provide Good Options**
- Make all options plausible
- Avoid obvious wrong answers

✅ **Test Your Quiz**
- Preview before publishing
- Check for typos and errors

### For Quiz Takers

✅ **Read Carefully**
- Take your time with each question
- Read all options before answering

✅ **Stay Consistent**
- Use the same name for accurate leaderboard tracking

✅ **Have Fun!**
- Don't stress about scores
- Learn from incorrect answers

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Fetch Threads Posts?
- Check the username is correct
- The Threads API may have limitations
- Try using the manual creation method instead

### Quiz Not Saving?
- Ensure all fields are filled
- Check that you have at least one question
- Make sure all options have text

### Not Seeing Your Quiz?
- Refresh the page
- Check you're logged in with the correct account
- Go to Profile → My Quizzes to verify

### Forgot Password?
- Currently, password reset is not available
- Create a new account if needed
- Future update will include password reset
